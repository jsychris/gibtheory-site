GIB Theory

Support

GIB Theory is a study app for Gibraltar's driving theory test — car and motorcycle.

Common questions

  • How do I switch between car and motorcycle? Almanac tab → choose Car or Motorcycle.
  • What is the Daily Five? Five questions a day from five different topics, on the Today tab. Your first attempt counts, and the month calendar shows the days you've done.
  • How do I reset my progress? Almanac tab → Reset all progress. This only affects your device — the app stores nothing anywhere else.
  • Is this an official DVLD app? No. It is an independent study aid based on the Gibraltar Highway Code 2022 and local road law. Rules change — always check gibraltar.gov.gi before your test.

Report a problem or a content error

Spotted a wrong answer, a typo, or a bug? Please email drivingtheoryapps@gmail.com with the question text or a screenshot — content corrections are treated as the highest priority and ship in the next update.
